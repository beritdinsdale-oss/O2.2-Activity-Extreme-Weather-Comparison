The activity uses official NOAA Earth from Orbit YouTube embeds and a curated summary of EPA Climate Change Indicators. It explicitly teaches that patterns of extreme weather are part of climate.

Comparisons:
1. Summer 2021, different regions: Pacific Northwest heat and Tropical Storm Claudette in the Southeast.
2. 2021, Pacific Northwest, different seasons: summer heat and a fall atmospheric river/coastal storm.
3. Long-term EPA indicators: heavy precipitation, heat/cold, drought/wildfire, and tropical cyclones.

Geographic caution:
The heat example includes western Oregon and the Willamette Valley. The atmospheric-river example is broader Pacific Northwest evidence. The activity does not treat all of Oregon as one uniform climate region.
