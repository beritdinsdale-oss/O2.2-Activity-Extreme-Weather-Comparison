Big idea

Climate includes patterns of extreme weather.

An individual event is weather. Patterns observed over many years are climate.

Extreme heat

Climate connection

Explore the connection: Select the steps in order.

Climate warms: Average temperatures rise over time.
Baseline shifts: Hot weather develops from a warmer starting point.
Extremes change: Very hot conditions become more likely and can become more intense.
Evidence meter: Strong

The connection between human-caused warming and increasing extreme heat is well established.

🌧️

Heavy precipitation

Climate connection

Explore the connection: Select the steps in order.

Air warms: A warmer atmosphere can hold more water vapor.
Moisture increases: More water can be available to storms when the right weather conditions develop.
Downpours can intensify: That added moisture can contribute to heavier precipitation.
Evidence meter: Strong

Heavy precipitation has increased across much of the United States, although the amount of change differs by region.

Climate pattern: Climate change can influence different extremes through different physical processes. Local geography and weather systems still help determine which event occurs in a particular place.

Winter ice storm

What scientists know—and are still studying

A warmer climate does not eliminate winter weather. Freezing rain can still occur when liquid precipitation reaches a surface that is below freezing.

What is less certain is how climate change will affect the frequency of ice storms in a specific region, including the Willamette Valley.

Drag or select each statement: Is it something scientists know, or something still being studied?

Evidence meter: Still evolving

Scientists have less confidence about future regional ice-storm frequency than they do about extreme heat.

Key takeaway

An individual extreme event is weather. Patterns in the types, timing, location, frequency, and intensity of extreme weather over many years are part of climate. As climate changes, the conditions in which weather develops also change.
